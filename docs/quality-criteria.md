# Quality and editorial criteria

A useful resource lets a learner make, mend, modify, draw, grow, cook or experimentally investigate something. Prefer one complete, credible project over a large inspiration feed.

## Acceptance

1. **Free instruction:** enough meaningful content to finish without buying a course. Record registration, software limits and optional paid extras separately. A materials purchase is not a tuition fee.
2. **An outcome:** a specific make, experiment, repaired object, design or completed digital exercise.
3. **Actionable steps:** diagrams, dimensions, demonstrations, examples or instructions appropriate to the learner.
4. **Credibility and attribution:** original creator, transparent process or a defensible educational organization. Community libraries require sample checks; hosting alone is not a quality guarantee.
5. **Reproducibility:** tools, parts and workspace can realistically be obtained. Disclose mandatory proprietary kits and specialist access.
6. **Self-paced access:** no required live attendance or fixed completion schedule for the linked offering. Safety supervision may still be necessary for particular activities.
7. **Transferable learning:** explain or practice a skill beyond unexplained product assembly.
8. **Practical accessibility:** describe supported difficulty, language, screen use, setup and risks. Use null when unknown.

Do not list mandatory paid-only courses, broken or incomplete tutorials, advertising with no instruction, unauthorized copies, or inspiration-only collections as checked recommendations. Preserve inherited questionable entries in `needs-review`, `reference-only`, or `excluded` with the reason. This keeps the migration auditable without passing a research lead off as a recommendation.

## Evidence levels

| Level | Required evidence | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| source-checked | Publisher, resource and free offering identified; label inherited Notion checks explicitly | A complete project was inspected or built |
| sample-reviewed | Complete representative instructions inspected for steps, setup, output and payment barriers; direct link and dated notes | The project was personally completed |
| build-tested | Actual project completion report, obstacles, substitutions and functional result, in addition to sample evidence | Every project in a library was tested |

Record current verification only against publisher evidence. An imported source date is kept in provenance; it does not become an independent verification date. Automated HTTP success does not establish free access, quality or safety. Reviews do not expire automatically, but dates let readers judge freshness.

## Dispositions

`listed` is a current checked offering. `needs-review` is a preserved candidate with unresolved claims. `reference-only` is useful background, discovery or planning material outside the complete-project standard. `excluded` records a confirmed failure of the criteria. Move a listing into review when significant contrary evidence appears; do not delete based on a single timeout or bot block.

Difficulty, cost bands, duration, workspace and safety are separate concerns. A free expert video may require an expensive workshop. “Beginner” must not suggest hazardous repairs are casual activities. Do not assign numerical quality scores or build-tested labels without evidence. See [repair safety](safety.md).
